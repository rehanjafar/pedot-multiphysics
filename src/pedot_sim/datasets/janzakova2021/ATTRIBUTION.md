Changes: selected experimental and image-derived data from Figures 2a, 2f,
2h, 3d, 4b and 4c; converted square millimetres to square metres; reshaped
columns; retained first CV cycles in acquisition order; added provenance and
a quality flag for the reported zero projected area at 10 Hz. No imputation,
curve digitization, model fitting or baseline subtraction. The source's
circuit simulations and fitted circuit parameters are excluded.

`source_metadata.json` records exact source and subset SHA-256 hashes, section
sizes, units, conditions and limitations. CSV row/column provenance is one
based and references the original semicolon-delimited `dataset.csv`.
